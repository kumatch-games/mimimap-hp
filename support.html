Minimap

Support

Minimap shows your surroundings as a circular map on iPhone and a paired Apple Watch. Choose a destination on iPhone to see its direction and straight-line distance.

Getting started

Allow location access while using the app. An internet connection is needed to download new map areas and search for places. Open the companion app on your paired Apple Watch to see the shared map and destination distance.

Location and direction

If your location appears inaccurate, check that Precise Location is enabled for Minimap in iPhone Settings. GPS and compass readings can vary with your surroundings. Distances are measured in a straight line, rather than along a walking route.

Arrival notifications

Notification permission is requested when destination guidance starts. You can change it in iPhone Settings. Notification delivery, sound, and vibration follow your device settings.

Apple Watch

Keep the watch paired with your iPhone. Destination selection and ending guidance are performed on iPhone. The watch uses its own heading to rotate the minimap, and receiving a new map may take time.

Purchases and restoring skins

Optional map skins are one-time purchases. Buy a skin from Settings on iPhone, then select it to use it on your iPhone and paired Apple Watch. The purchase button shows the price for your App Store region.

To restore a previous purchase, use the same Apple Account for App Store purchases and choose Restore Purchases in Minimap’s Settings. A purchase awaiting approval unlocks after approval. If product information cannot load, check your internet connection and try again.

Contact

For questions or problems, use the contact form below. Please include your app version, device model, OS version, and a description of the problem. You can enter an email address if you would like a reply. Avoid including personal information or location details that you do not want to share.
