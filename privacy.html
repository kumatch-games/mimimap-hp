Minimap

Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how Minimap uses information to show nearby maps, guide you toward a destination, and connect your iPhone with your Apple Watch.

Minimap is provided by kumatch games. You do not need an account to use the app.

Location and heading

With your permission, Minimap uses your device’s location and heading to center and rotate the map, calculate the direction and straight-line distance to your destination, and detect arrival.

On iPhone, location updates continue in the background during active destination guidance. They stop when you end guidance or arrival is detected. You can change location permissions, including Precise Location, in your device’s Settings.

Map downloads and place search

To download nearby OpenStreetMap data, the current app sends the coordinates of the surrounding map area to the public Overpass API service at overpass-api.de. These coordinates can reveal your location. The service also receives network information needed to process the request, including your IP address.

Overpass API documentation describes IP-based rate limiting, and its server documentation describes request logging. The service operator controls server records and their retention. Minimap’s provider does not operate these public servers.

Place search and search suggestions use Apple MapKit. Requests include the text you enter and may include a nearby region to help find relevant results. Apple’s handling of its services is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Information stored on your devices

  • Your display settings, saved destinations, and navigation state are stored in the app’s local storage.
  • Downloaded map areas, including their center coordinates, are cached locally. The iPhone cache removes expired or excess entries as it is used.
  • The iPhone and paired Apple Watch exchange location, destination, map, and display settings through Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. The watch stores the latest received state and map locally.

Minimap does not record a continuous history of your movements. Your device’s backup settings may affect whether local app data is included in a backup.

Notifications

Minimap requests notification permission when you start destination guidance so it can deliver an arrival notification. Arrival notifications are scheduled locally on your devices. You can change notification permissions in Settings.

Advertising and analytics

The current app includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDK, and does not perform cross-app advertising tracking. Map and search services still receive the requests described above.

Contacting us

If you submit the contact form, we receive the inquiry type and content you provide, and an email address if you choose to request a reply. We use this information to handle your inquiry. Please avoid including location details or other personal information that is unnecessary for your question.

The form is hosted by Google Forms. Google handles information according to Google’s Privacy Policy. You can use the same form to ask about information you have sent us or request its deletion.

Your choices

You can end destination guidance and delete saved destinations in the app. To remove the app’s local data, delete the app from your iPhone and the companion app from your watch. Existing device backups are managed separately through your device and backup settings.

This website

This site uses no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or external fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub processes connection information when you visit it according to the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and revise the date shown above. For questions about this policy, use the contact form.
